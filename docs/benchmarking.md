# Current benchmark and design reference

Reviewed 12 September 2026. This is a feature/assurance benchmark against published primary sources. It is not a head-to-head clinical trial, product endorsement, proprietary-system test or global ranking. NCDAI's ambition is translated into testable requirements rather than unsupported percentile claims.

| Reference | Published capability or principle | NCDAI requirement and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [UpToDate Expert AI](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/en/solutions/uptodate/ai-clinical-decision-support) | Vendor describes clinician-authored evidence supporting AI answers and clinical workflows. | Versioned evidence registry, inspectable sources, reviewer ownership and grounded model inputs. Clinical coverage remains much narrower than the reference. |
| [ClinicalKey AI](https://www.elsevier.com/en-in/products/clinicalkey/clinicalkey-ai) | Vendor describes cited answers, licensed content and EHR/API integration. | Traceable evidence, licensed/authorized content only, explicit EMR contract tests and no unsupported live-integration claim. |
| [Epic AI workflow tools](https://www.epic.com/epic/post/epic-ai-charting-rolls-out-alongside-an-expanding-set-of-built-in-ai-capabilities/) | Vendor describes chart-context summaries and AI embedded in clinician workflows. | Longitudinal patient view, reuse of structured data, reviewable summaries and explicit write-back authority. No equivalent-scale claim. |
| [OpenMRS](https://openmrs.org/product/) | Open platform publishes REST/FHIR interoperability and clinical documentation capabilities. | Vendor-neutral resource contracts, stable IDs, coded measurements and portable data rather than dependence on one EMR. |
| [WHO SMART Guidelines](https://www.who.int/teams/digital-health-and-innovation/smart-guidelines/) | Guideline digitization links recommendations, data dictionaries, decision logic and testable requirements. | Stable rule IDs, explicit data semantics and guideline-to-test traceability. Do not describe an NCDAI rule set as WHO certified. |
| [Kenya Core FHIR](https://fhir.dha.go.ke/ig/index.html) | DHA publishes national base profiles and terminology foundations. | FHIR R4 foundation and documented national-profile conformance work; deployment target/version must be confirmed with DHA. |
| [SMART App Launch](https://www.hl7.org/fhir/smart-app-launch/) and [CDS Hooks](https://cds-hooks.hl7.org/) | Standard patterns for authorized patient-context launch and workflow-triggered support. | Adapter design supports these patterns when the host implements them; published capability is not evidence Aifya/QAfya support them. |
| [NICE evidence framework](https://www.nice.org.uk/corporate/ecd7) | Evidence needs follow intended purpose and risk. | Separate software correctness from clinical effectiveness, workflow value, cost and later evaluation. |
| [NHS DCB0129](https://digital.nhs.uk/data-and-information/information-standards/governance/latest-activity/standards-and-collections/dcb0129-clinical-risk-management-its-application-in-the-manufacture-of-health-it-systems) | Clinical risk management for health IT manufacture. | Named hazards, mitigations, residual risks, test evidence and a clinical safety owner; a reference model, not a Kenyan legal requirement or a certification claim. |
| [ONC decision-support interventions](https://healthit.gov/test-method/decision-support-interventions/) | Source attributes and intervention risk-management transparency. | Record intended use, provenance, performance limits, model/rule versions and maintenance responsibilities. Used as an international design reference. |
| [OWASP ASVS](https://github.com/OWASP/ASVS/tree/v5.0.0) | Verifiable application security controls. | Authentication, session/CSRF protection, authorization, validation, redacted logging and negative tests. Scope-tested controls do not equal full ASVS certification. |

Commercial products above were not accessed behind paid accounts, benchmarked on our cases, or used as sources of licensed clinical content. Their marketing superlatives are not adopted as facts. The appropriate future comparison is a prespecified independent study of NCDAI, a rules-only baseline and usual care/available alternatives under the same case and workflow conditions.

The immediate engineering benchmark uses distinct synthetic case workflows, software threat scenarios, evidence-integrity checks, browser checks and database recovery/concurrency checks. Results must name the build, environment, case set, AI mode and external-integration status. Passing them establishes only the tested engineering behavior.
