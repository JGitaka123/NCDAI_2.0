# Evaluation implementation budget and assumptions

Draft 0.1 · 12 September 2026 · Planning estimates in USD, not supplier quotes, award commitments or a verified currency conversion. Prices are assumed fully loaded planning rates; the finance lead must confirm tax treatment, employment costs, procurement and funder allowability. This is the single source for totals in the concept and protocols.

The base scenario supports 12 proposed facilities in three counties over 12 months, 720 shadow encounters and 1,200 active index participants, with a 300-person follow-up subset. It assumes a locally led award and an independently led evaluation. Existing DHA/ethical approvals are owner-confirmed; reference capture is administrative. The budget does not charge again for obtaining approvals already held.

The budget does not pay clinicians to accept recommendations. Protected time covers orientation, interviews and study activities, distinct from salaried research posts. Safety coordination, independent adjudication and third-review work must have defined deliverables to avoid duplicate payment. Hosting/provider lines are spending ceilings, not current price claims. The implementation lead's work is installation, support and evaluation fidelity of a completed product; new features/development are outside this request. Existing devices or donated staff time reduce cash requirements; replacement is not assumed automatically. Participant reimbursements follow existing approved institutional arrangements and must not be coercive.

The base laboratory allowance assumes 150 of the 300 follow-up participants require HbA1c assessment; actual case mix and clinically indicated safety tests may change costs. Standard clinical care, medicines and necessary urgent treatment remain funded through the responsible care arrangements; confirm how uninsured costs are handled before recruitment. Additional clinical testing cannot be withheld because a research allowance is exhausted.

The expected AI volume includes repeat and controlled evaluation calls; ordinary C-arm exposure is only approximately 400 index participants. Report actual token/model/latency use and avoid presenting the unused ceiling as a cost per routine patient. No LLM pretraining or fine-tuning is budgeted.

## Allocation, sensitivity and reconciliation

The proposed contracting plan locates the PI, research, clinical, procurement and indirect-cost recipients in SSA. Only the assumedUSD 5,200 hosting/AI lines are provisionally classified outside SSA; on that assumption 98.71% of the evaluation request flows to SSA recipients. This is an allocation scenario, not proof of signed partners or ultimate supply-chain location. Track legal recipient, country, subaward and downstream vendor payments separately and verify the actual future call's flow rule; set an internal planning floor of 90% to retain margin against the old 80% requirement.

Apply uniform direct-cost sensitivity of−15% and+20%, retaining the illustrative 10% indirect rate: **USD 343,332–484,704**, baseUSD 403,920. These are alternative scenarios, not an extra contingency added to the request. Study scope, safety staffing and follow-up may constrain reductions. Reprice changed recruitment, more sites, additional dose modules or an EMR evaluation as explicit amendments rather than absorbing them invisibly.

Readiness plus base evaluation would total **USD 455,920**, but only **USD 403,920** is the proposed evaluation request. Do not place the combined figure in the grant-total field. Use a controlled budget version, one currency/price date and one FTE per role; automatically or independently reconcile every narrative, template, partner schedule, indirect base and total. Enter approved actual exchange rate/source/date only when producing a KES or funder-currency budget. Original application budget discrepancies cannot be reconciled from the current draft without the original workbook; no historical figure is treated as established here.
