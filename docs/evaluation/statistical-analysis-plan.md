# Statistical analysis plan

Draft 0.1 · 12 September 2026 · To be signed and timestamped by the independent statistician and PI before the relevant database is unlocked. This plan is not a claim that data have been collected or that an impact trial is powered.

## Design, populations and estimands

Validation comprises 300 representative cases, 300 acute challenges and 300 dose/withholding challenges. Pilot feasibility comprises 12 proposed facilities in three counties, allocated 4 each to usual care(A), rules/dose support(B), or the same support plus constrained AI briefing(C). A 720-encounter shadow baseline precedes a 1,200-person active cohort: 100 consecutive eligible index participants per facility. Only the first active consultation per participant contributes to the index-consultation analysis; follow-up observations are nested within participant and facility. Eligibility, exclusions, module manifest and observation windows are frozen before analysis.

The primary pilot estimand is the equal-facility-weighted mean proportion of eligible enrolled index consultations at assigned intervention facilities(B+C) that obtain a completed, clinician-reviewed NCDAI record during the consultation, regardless of whether the clinician uses the model. Each facility denominator contains all its enrolled index consultations, including outages, non-use and unsuccessful completions; the target is 800 across eight intervention facilities. Report the participant-weighted proportion separately and describe B and C before pooling. Do not count only successful app sessions. Document same-day later completion separately; it does not satisfy the during-consultation endpoint.

Clinical process estimands are encounter-level differences in guideline-concordant management B−A and C−B under assignment, and C−A as a secondary contrast. Concordance means all independently adjudicated mandatory actions were appropriately addressed, without an adjudicated contraindicated action; acceptable clinical alternatives qualify. Report individual components and reference-indeterminate cases. These are exploratory estimates in a feasibility study, not confirmatory superiority tests. Pure software safety denominators remain separate from clinician decision and patient outcome denominators.

## Endpoint definitions

| Endpoint | Numerator / measure | Denominator / time |
|---|---|---|
| Acute sensitivity | Cases receiving every required minimum acute escalation/action | Evaluable acute reference-positive cases; locked 300-case challenge stratum |
| Harmful dose decision | Adjudicated harmful offered dose, harmful omission or failure to withhold/escalate under the prespecified rubric | All evaluable 300 dose/withholding challenges; additionally report 150 dose-eligible and 150 withholding subgroups |
| Correct dose/withholding | All required fields within approved clinical alternatives / appropriate explicit abstention | Relevant eligible/ineligible stratum, not all 900 pooled |
| App completion | Complete reviewed index record within consultation | All enrolled index encounters in B/C, failed attempts retained |
| Clinical process concordance | Reference-defined mandatory actions completed/documented without contraindicated action | Independently evaluated index consultations, by assigned arm; unavailable records counted unsuccessful in primary analysis |
| Referral completion | Verified arrival/assessment at intended receiving service | All referrals indicated by independent reference; also show among referrals actually issued; at 14 and 30 days, without implying these are acceptable emergency delays |
| Burden | Consultation elapsed minutes, documentation minutes, repeated manual fields and interruptions | Stratified observed sessions: 20 per facility at baseline and 20 during active phase, total 480 planned |
| Safety | Possibly/probably/definitely intervention-related serious events, near misses and material errors, separately | All exposed participants; report per 1,000 participants and per 1,000 encounters, by exposure and assignment |
| Clinical measurements | SBP change in mmHg; HbA1c change in percentage points | Prespecified HTN/T2D follow-up subsets at 12 weeks ±2 weeks; individual conditions and joint multimorbidity reported separately |
| Equity and coverage | Completion, availability, errors, burden and referral results | Prespecified sex/gender where approved, age 18–44/45–64/65+, rurality, resource level, language, cadre and disease groups; unknown data retained |

Total consultation time includes preparation, ordinary records, app interaction, waits and re-entry after the patient leaves. App event duration alone is not total workload. Observer-coded time definitions are calibrated on a separate sample; report observation refusals and interobserver agreement. Reference-standard reviewers receive clinical material with allocation/app branding redacted where feasible; record occasions when blinding is broken.

## Precision and sample-size examples

For zero observed failures in n independent trials, the one-sided 95% exact upper failure bound is `1 − 0.05^(1/n)`. At n=150 it is 1.98%; at 300 it is 0.994%. Thus zero failures in the 150 dose-offer subgroup does not establish an error risk below 1%. With 300/300 acute detections, the corresponding one-sided 95% lower sensitivity bound is 99.006%. These describe the specific test distribution; case-family dependence and enrichment prevent generalization to population safety. Report two-sided 95% exact intervals for ordinary descriptive estimates, with the one-sided bounds explicitly labelled for safety gates.

For app completion at an assumed 85% across 800 encounters in eight intervention facilities of 100 each, an illustrative design effect `1+(m−1)ICC` is 1.99/5.95/10.9 for ICC 0.01/0.05/0.10. Approximate effective samples are 402/134/73, giving normal-approximation half-widths 3.49/6.04/8.17 percentage points. These calculations illustrate clustering loss; they are not exact small-cluster intervals or proof of pilot power. Primary reporting includes all facility proportions and cluster-aware uncertainty rather than treating 800 observations as independent.

For a **future**, two-arm individually independent SBP trial, an assumed SD 15 mmHg, target difference 5 mmHg, two-sided alpha 0.05 and 80% power give approximately 142 participants per arm using `2(z0.975+z0.8)^2 SD²/difference²`. A rough planning expansion using mean cluster size 50, ICC 0.03 and 20% loss gives about 437 per arm, or nine 50-person clusters per arm. This is a lower-level illustration, **not the definitive sample size**: few-cluster degrees of freedom, variable cluster sizes, baseline correlation, recruitment, repeated measures and cluster-time effects require simulation before a real impact study. Do not transfer SBP assumptions to HbA1c or dichotomize both merely to reuse one formula.

## Pilot estimation and inference

Report recruitment, completion, missingness, errors and resource use by facility, county and arm. Primary feasibility summaries weight facilities equally for the facility-average estimand and also show participant-weighted estimates; label the two explicitly. Use a prespecified beta-binomial or cluster-level model for interval estimation and show an unmodelled facility table. With only four facilities per arm, cluster bootstrap and asymptotic mixed-model P-values can be unstable; do not advertise precise causal conclusions from them.

For exploratory randomised comparisons, use cluster-level summaries adjusted for baseline outcome and county/selected balancing factors, with randomisation inference based on the actual permitted allocation set. Report randomisation-compatible intervals where estimable and unadjusted differences; limited allocation resolution must be visible. A supportive individual-level mixed model may include treatment, baseline value, calendar period, county and prespecified case-mix variables, with facility and participant intercepts for longitudinal outcomes; assess convergence and do not substitute an unstable model for the main descriptive results. Provider downtime/non-use stays in assignment analyses. As-treated estimates are secondary and potentially confounded.

The primary clinical measurement is continuous SBP in participants with hypertension; HbA1c is a separate continuous outcome for T2D. Adjust for baseline measurement and report clinically interpretable units. Binary control outcomes are secondary and use the locally approved thresholds, with full definitions specified before data lock. No multiplicity-adjusted confirmatory claims are planned; all exploratory confidence intervals are labelled accordingly. Qualitative themes and mechanism associations explain implementation, without presenting postrandomisation mediator associations as proven causal mediation.

## Missingness, fairness, economics and integrity

Record missingness by reason, arm, facility and time. Operational failure/non-use is part of the feasibility endpoint, not missing-at-random. For clinical follow-up, present complete-case results and missing-outcome proportions, then multiple imputation only if assumptions are defensible using baseline predictors, treatment and cluster structure. Sensitivity analyses include plausible arm-specific deterioration among missing participants and inverse-probability weighting where appropriate. Death/hospitalisation is a safety outcome and should not be imputed as ordinary missing SBP/HbA1c; report it explicitly with a prespecified survivor-estimand limitation.

Prespecified subgroup results are descriptive with denominators and intervals. Use interaction estimates for between-group differences rather than comparing separate significance tests. Suppress potentially identifying tiny cells; do not claim fairness when a group is underrepresented. Oversampled challenge groups retain their own weights/labels. Report language and connectivity failures even when no clinical output is produced.

Economics use Kenyan provider/health-system and patient perspectives separately over the 12-month evaluation. Record resource quantities and actual local prices at a stated price date; distinguish research-only costs, implementation costs and recurrent operation. Compare B−A and C−B in cost-consequence tables and, if suitable, incremental cost per additional concordant encounter or completed indicated referral. No QALY/DALY or national cost-effectiveness claim without measured/modelled outcomes and explicit validated assumptions. Explore usage, hosting, staff time, travel and medicine/lab prices; no discounting is necessary for a one-year within-study horizon. [CHEERS 2022](https://www.bmj.com/content/376/bmj-2021-067975).

Maintain a data dictionary, analysis-code version, blinded queries and signed lock record. All analysis deviations are dated and classified before/after unblinding. An independent statistician reproduces primary tables, case counts and budget totals. Publish anonymized or synthetic reproducibility materials within permissions; patient-level access remains governed. Current reporting uses [CONSORT 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03635-5) with the [pilot extension](https://www.bmj.com/content/355/bmj.i5239), not an unqualified claim that a feasibility trial establishes efficacy.
